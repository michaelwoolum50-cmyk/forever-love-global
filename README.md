# Forever Love — Your Daily Relationship Companion

**Check in, connect, and grow together.**

Forever Love is the relationship app for couples who want to stay deeply connected every single day. Powered by Ember — your personal AI relationship companion — it helps you nurture your love with daily check-ins, shared goals, and meaningful conversations that keep your bond strong.

## Features
- Daily relationship check-ins with mood tracking
- Ember AI companion for personalized relationship guidance
- Shared goal setting and milestone tracking
- Conversation starters tailored to your relationship stage
- Date night planner with AI-suggested activities
- Love language discovery and compatibility insights
- Private couples journal with shared entries
- Anniversary and special date reminders
- Relationship health dashboard
